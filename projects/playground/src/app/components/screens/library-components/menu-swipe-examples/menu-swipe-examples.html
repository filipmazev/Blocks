Swipe and context actions

Use a horizontal two-finger trackpad gesture, touch swipe, or mouse drag. Right-click and Shift+F10 expose the same commands. Vertical scrolling remains native. On a trackpad, let multiple actions finish appearing, ease off and push outward again to stretch the edge action. You can also start a fresh swipe anywhere on the item. Reveal follows your swipe directly. Stretching beyond reveal resists more at first and becomes easier as you continue. Drag back before the commit threshold to cancel. Trackpads activate at that threshold; pointer and touch drags activate on release beyond it; reverse to cancel. Close the exposed side before opening the other.

Compare the equal-sized Archive and Delete rows below: Both use the same strong stretch resistance and reveal the same way. Wider rows reduce extra resistance to keep the gesture manageable. When stretching toward activation, Delete resists strongly at the start, moderately in the middle and very little near the end. Reverse the stretch to cancel. Archive and Delete simulate API requests: the stretched action keeps its spinner while pending, exposes Retry on failure, then removes the row on success. Turn off “Remove completed rows” to animate closed instead. Download always collapses after success.
